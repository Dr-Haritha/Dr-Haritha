# SOP: FAQ Writing Guidelines

---

## Purpose

This Standard Operating Procedure defines the standards and best practices for writing FAQ (Frequently Asked Questions) sections across all organisation-facing content, including website articles, product documentation, support pages, and marketing materials.

Adherence to this guide ensures that FAQs are clear, consistent, discoverable, and aligned with the brand voice. Any team member tasked with creating or reviewing FAQs should follow this document.

---

## 1. Structure

### 1.1 Question Order

FAQs must be presented in a logical sequence that mirrors the user's journey, beginning with foundational questions and progressing toward advanced topics.

**FAQ Sequence — For articles:**

Follow this order when structuring FAQ sections:

What it is → Why it matters → Core principles → Strategies → Case studies → Measuring performance

This mirrors the user's natural learning journey from foundational understanding to practical application.

- Start with the most common questions. Place the highest-frequency questions at the top.
- Follow a basic → advanced flow. Guide users from introductory concepts to more nuanced topics.

**Examples:**
- Q: What is [product/service]?
- Q: Who should consider using [product/service]?
- Q: How is [product/service] different from other tools in this category?

### 1.2 Question Format

All questions must use interrogative (question-based) phrasing using 'W' words or auxiliary verbs.

**Preferred openers:** What, Who, How, Why, When, Do, Is, Can, Should

**Examples:**
- Q: Who should consider using this platform?
- Q: Do we need to replace our existing tools?
- Q: Is there a free trial available?

### 1.3 Quantity and Grouping

Limit FAQs to only the most useful and relevant questions.

**For articles:**

All FAQs within an article or page must be tied to the primary keyword of that article. Do not include questions that address different topics or secondary keywords — those belong in separate FAQ sections on their own pages.

Group related questions into named categories when there are many FAQs.

**Suggested categories:** Pricing, Features, Integrations, Troubleshooting, Getting Started.

---

## 2. Content

### 2.1 Clarity and Conciseness

Answers must be direct and easy to understand. The standard target length is 100 words or 1–3 sentences per answer.

- Be clear and direct. Every answer should immediately address the question.
- Solve the user's problem. Each answer should provide a clear solution, not just a description.

**Example:**

> Q: Is there a free trial?
>
> A: Yes. Free trials are available for a limited time to new users. Register now to get visibility insights and recommended actions.

### 2.2 Helpful Detail

**Primary Keyword Note — For Articles:**

When listing tools, platforms, or examples in an answer, do not place the primary keyword inside parentheses (). Parentheses reduce the keyword's weight in both search and AI signals. Keep the primary keyword prominent in the answer text itself.

Include supporting details such as links, step-by-step instructions, or examples.

**Example:**

> Q: Do we need to replace our existing tools?
>
> A: No. Your existing tools (analytics platforms, CRMs, SEO tools, etc.) measure the visible funnel. This platform integrates with your current stack and measures additional signals that influence decisions before a user clicks.

### 2.3 Content Standardisation

- **Avoid repetition.** Do not restate information already covered in another FAQ.
- **Update regularly.** Review all FAQs whenever the product, pricing, or policies change.

## 3. Voice & Tone

### 3.1 Active Voice

All FAQs must be written in an active voice. Questions and answers should feel natural and conversational.

| ✔ Correct | ✖ Incorrect |
|---|---|
| Do we need to replace our existing tools? | Do our existing tools need to be replaced? |
| How does the platform measure performance? | How is performance measured by the platform? |

### 3.2 User Perspective

Write questions from the perspective of the user. Use the same language they use in emails, chat messages, or support tickets.

- Use "our", "we", and "I" when framing the user's situation.
- Avoid internal jargon or organisation-centric phrasing.

### 3.3 Language and Brand Voice

- Use simple language. Avoid technical jargon unless it is the user's known vocabulary.
- Be helpful and reassuring. The goal is to assist users, not to impress them with complexity.
- Stay consistent with the brand voice. FAQs must match the tone of the rest of the website or documentation.

---

## 4. Formatting

### 4.1 Visual Hierarchy

- Questions in bold or larger font. This allows users to scan the FAQ list at a glance.
- Answers in regular weight. Keep visual contrast between Q and A clear.
- Use bullet points or numbered lists for multi-step answers or lists of items.

### 4.2 Length

The standard answer length is 1–3 short sentences, or a maximum of 100 words. Exceptions are permitted when a topic genuinely requires more detail (e.g., technical integrations), but these should be rare.

### 4.3 Consistency

- Bold key terms within answers.
- Maintain consistent punctuation and capitalisation across all FAQs in the same document.
- Follow a single style guide for all FAQ sections — punctuation at end of questions, capitalisation of question words.

---

## 5. Usability & SEO

### 5.1 Searchability

FAQs should be discoverable both through on-page search and external search engines. Write questions the way users would actually type them into Google or an AI assistant.

Use keywords naturally. Include terms people search for within the question itself.

| ✔ Write This | ✖ Not This |
|---|---|
| Is there a free trial available? | Free trial? |
| How do I connect the platform to my existing tools? | Integration? |

### 5.2 Internal Linking

Where relevant, direct users to additional resources using internal links. This reduces support load and improves overall site navigation.

- Link to detailed articles, product features, or related FAQ categories.
- Use descriptive anchor text. Example: "See our integration guide" rather than "click here".

### 5.3 Mobile Friendliness

All FAQ pages must be tested and optimised for mobile screens. Ensure answers are readable on smaller viewports without horizontal scrolling.

### 5.4 Structured Data Markup

Where technically feasible, implement FAQ schema markup (structured data) on FAQ pages. This enables search engines to display FAQ rich results directly in search engine results pages (SERPs), increasing organic visibility without requiring additional clicks.

## Quick Checklist

- [ ] Questions are ordered following the recommended sequence: What / Importance / Principles / Strategies / Case Studies / Measuring / FAQs
- [ ] Questions are ordered from most common to most advanced within each group
- [ ] All questions use interrogative phrasing (What, Who, How, Do, Is, etc.)
- [ ] Related questions are grouped into clear categories
- [ ] All FAQs are tied to the primary keyword of the article or page. No off-topic questions included
- [ ] Answers are 100 words or fewer (exceptions documented)
- [ ] Active voice is used throughout
- [ ] Questions reflect the user's perspective and language
- [ ] No jargon unless it is the user's known vocabulary
- [ ] The primary keyword does not appear inside parentheses () in any answer
- [ ] Key terms are bolded
- [ ] Formatting is consistent
- [ ] No duplicate information
- [ ] Internal links included where relevant
- [ ] Content has been reviewed for accuracy and is up to date
